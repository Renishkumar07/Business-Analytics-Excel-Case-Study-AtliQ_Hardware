## 🔍 The Problem

- AtliQ Hardware struggled with **inconsistent sales results** and **unclear profit margins** across different products, business divisions, and geographic markets.
- Data was **scattered across multiple sources**, making it difficult to get a complete picture for decision-making.
- The company needed a way to monitor important performance metrics in sales and finance to improve operations and plan for growth.

---

## 🏢 Background

AtliQ Hardware operates in multiple countries and markets, selling various products through different business divisions and distributors. However, the company faced challenges including inconsistent sales results, unmet sales targets, and unclear profit margins. To address these issues, the company needed detailed data analysis to support decision-making and future planning.

---

## 💡 Solution

This project addresses these challenges through two focused analyses:

1. **Sales Analytics** — Examines customer and market performance over a three-year period (2019 – 2021).
2. **Finance Analytics** — Analyzes profitability and financial performance using Profit & Loss (P&L) reports for the same three years.

> ⚠️ **Important Note:** AtliQ Hardware operates on a fiscal year that runs from **September through August (Sep – Aug)**.

## 🛠️ Skills & Tools Used

### Technical Skills

| Skill | Description |
|---|---|
| **Data Preparation** | Cleaned and organized raw data using ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes. |
| **Power Query** | Built a date table and calculated fiscal months and quarters for accurate time-based analysis. |
| **Power Pivot** | Created and organized data relationships to support complex analysis. |
| **DAX** | Developed formulas to calculate key performance metrics, including the difference between actual and target sales. |
| **Data Visualization** | Used conditional formatting to make data easier to understand and interpret. |

### Soft Skills

- 🎯 **User-Focused Design** — Built reports that directly address business needs and are easy to use.
- 📈 **Strategic Planning** — Designed solutions that can grow and adapt as the business evolves.
- ✅ **Quality Control** — Refined reports to ensure clarity and accuracy in all outputs.
- 💼 **Business Knowledge** — Applied expertise in sales and finance to identify insights that matter to the business.
